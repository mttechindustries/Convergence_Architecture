> **The system is not one machine. It is the graph between machines.**
>
> A camera may observe skin reflectance. Radar may recover micromotion. Wi-Fi may reveal motion or pose. A device graph may supply identity. A drone may improve geometry. An AI model may combine partial signals. Another system may select an action and measure what happens next. The architecture emerges in the connections.

**Convergence Architecture** is a public research program developed by **Marc Tuinier at MT Tech Industries LLC**. It maps how independently developed sensing, identity, inference, mobility, actuation, and adaptive-computing capabilities can become a distributed human-facing system—and specifies the evidence required to distinguish technical reachability from bounded deployment.

## The research object

<p align="center">
  <img src="docs/visuals/02-adaptive-loop.svg" alt="Adaptive convergence loop from sensing through model updating" width="100%">
</p>

```text
SENSE
  → IDENTIFY OR LINK
  → INFER STATE OR INTENT
  → POSITION
  → SELECT OR DELIVER AN ACTION
  → MEASURE RESPONSE
  → UPDATE THE MODEL
```

The central question is not whether each stage exists. Many do.

The central questions are:

1. **Which edges are physically and computationally reachable?**
2. **Which edges have been demonstrated in controlled systems?**
3. **Which stages are documented together in one bounded deployment?**
4. **What records establish persistence, identity linkage, response-driven adaptation, and operator intent?**

A distributed system can divide those functions among fixed infrastructure, access points, cameras, radar, wearables, vehicles, unmanned aircraft, edge processors, remote services, data brokers, automated models, and human operators. No single node requires complete visibility or complete control.

## Read the architecture in three minutes

### 1. The body is already a signal environment

Breathing displaces tissue. Cardiac activity creates micromotion and optical variation. Muscles generate electrical signals before audible speech. The body emits heat, alters radio propagation, reflects radar, produces sound and vibration, and responds to externally delivered energy.

The sensing boundary therefore does not begin at an implant. It includes the physical environment around the body.

### 2. Ordinary infrastructure can acquire secondary functions

A communication field can become a measurement field. A camera can become a physiological sensor. Headphones can become a neuromuscular interface. A drone can become a movable sensing, relay, geometry, or actuation node. A retained measurement can become more identifying as analytical capability improves.

The declared purpose of infrastructure does not exhaust the functions its physical configuration makes possible.

### 3. Partial observables become more powerful when fused

One sensor may provide location, another respiration, another activity, another device association, and another historical context. Artificial intelligence does not need a perfect channel when it can combine several weak channels.

The architecture is therefore a **division-of-labour system**, not a search for one omnipotent device.

### 4. Governance breaks where measurement exceeds representation

Ambient systems can measure people who do not exist as entities in the protocol performing the measurement. They have no address, credential, session role, consent object, termination primitive, or place in a subject-level protocol log.

That structural mismatch is the **Representation Gap**.

## Evidence is a ladder, not a mood

<p align="center">
  <img src="docs/visuals/03-evidence-ladder.svg" alt="Evidence ladder from E0 observation through E7 strategic intent" width="100%">
</p>

| State | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| `E0` | Question, testimony, raw observation, or integration hypothesis |
| `E1` | Physical or formal mechanism established |
| `E2` | Laboratory or controlled capability demonstrated |
| `E3` | Patent or commercial product exists |
| `E4` | Institutional procurement, fielding, deployment, or normative adoption documented |
| `E5` | Cross-domain integration documented in one bounded system |
| `E6` | Scale, persistence, identity linkage, or retention documented |
| `E7` | Strategic intent, command, payment, coordination, or direction directly evidenced |

### The non-collapse rules

```text
CAPABILITY ≠ DEPLOYMENT
DEPLOYMENT ≠ INTEGRATION
INTEGRATION ≠ SCALE OR IDENTITY LINKAGE
SCALE OR IDENTITY LINKAGE ≠ INTENT
TECHNICAL COHERENCE ≠ EVENT-SPECIFIC ATTRIBUTION
NON-VERIFICATION ≠ NEGATION
PRESERVATION ≠ INDEPENDENT CORROBORATION
```

Evidence state, confidence, technical reachability, source access, and claim importance are independent variables. A pathway can be physically credible and evidentially open at the same time.

## Governance without compulsory identification

<p align="center">
  <img src="docs/visuals/04-governance-map.svg" alt="Device, space, session, and record-level governance around a measured person absent from the protocol namespace" width="100%">
</p>

The obvious response to an unrepresented measured person is to identify and register that person. That response creates the very identity infrastructure the protection is meant to prevent.

The public governance work therefore relocates policy to objects the system already represents:

- **Device:** disclose capability and visibly indicate active sensing.
- **Space:** attach machine-readable limits to a physical volume rather than an occupant.
- **Session:** bind initiator, purpose, configuration, responder set, proxy relationship, and time interval into a signed attestation.
- **Record:** enforce provenance, bounded retention, deletion, derivation constraints, and reassessment as analytical capability changes.

This approach grants protection by presence rather than enrolment. It does not claim to constrain a passive external observer executing no compliant protocol; that adversarial tail requires physical or legal controls.

## The open proof layers

The architecture is strongest when its unresolved layers are stated precisely.

### To establish `E5` — integrated operation

A bounded record must connect named sensing, identity or linkage, inference, action selection, actuation, response measurement, and model updating within one system or operation.

### To establish `E6` — scale and persistence

Records must show durable identity linkage, repeated operation, retention, cross-context continuity, fleet or population scale, or persistent person-model construction.

### To establish `E7` — strategic direction

Evidence must identify operator intent, mission, command, payment, access, procurement purpose, coordination, or explicit strategic instruction.

Architecture-level compatibility cannot substitute for those records. Neither can the absence of public access erase private observations; access status and corroboration status remain separate.

## Contributing evidence

Contributions are evaluated by what they establish, not by whether they appear supportive or skeptical.

A useful submission identifies:

1. the exact claim;
2. the primary source;
3. the evidence state supported;
4. the boundary the source does not cross;
5. competing explanations or contradictory evidence;
6. the next record that would materially change the conclusion.

Do not submit personal identifying information, private medical records, targeting instructions, evasion guidance, or unsupported accusations against named people or organizations.

See **[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)**.

# Primary Source Starter

This index is a public starting set, not the complete controlled evidence ledger. Each record is listed with the layer it can support and the claim it cannot establish alone.

## Radio-frequency hearing and speech-oriented patents

### Allan H. Frey — microwave auditory research

Early experimental literature established that pulsed radio-frequency energy can produce auditory sensations under specific exposure conditions.

**Supports:** physical mechanism and exposure-response research.  
**Does not alone support:** intelligible speech, field deployment, targeting, or event attribution.

### US6470214B1 — *Method and Device for Implementing the Radio Frequency Hearing Effect*

Assigned to the United States Department of the Air Force. Describes preprocessing and signal design intended to produce intelligible subjective sound through the RF-hearing pathway.

**Supports:** patent and engineering-design layer.  
**Does not alone support:** successful independent reproduction, procurement, deployment, or a specific event.

### US6587729B2 — *Apparatus for Audibly Communicating Speech Using the Radio Frequency Hearing Effect*

Related Air Force-assigned apparatus patent.

**Supports:** patent-family and apparatus-design layer.  
**Does not alone support:** field performance or operational use.

## Silent speech and neuromuscular interfaces

### *Wireless Silent Speech Interface Using Multi-Channel Textile EMG Sensors Integrated into Headphones* (2025)

Describes textile electrodes integrated into headphones and classification of a limited voice-free command vocabulary.

**Supports:** controlled wearable silent-speech capability and discreet form-factor transition.  
**Does not alone support:** unrestricted language reconstruction or contactless thought reading.

### *SilentWear: an Ultra-Low Power Wearable System for EMG-based Silent Speech Recognition* (2026)

Describes a textile neck interface, on-device inference, limited command recognition, and personalized recalibration.

**Supports:** continuous-wear, edge-processing, and personalized decoder capability.  
**Does not alone support:** unrestricted inner speech, covert remote acquisition, or general semantic decoding.

## Wi-Fi, radar, and ambient sensing

### *Person-in-WiFi: Fine-grained Person Perception Using WiFi*

Demonstrates body segmentation and pose-related inference from Wi-Fi measurements in controlled indoor environments.

**Supports:** communications-field-to-human-observation transition.  
**Does not alone support:** universal cross-building performance, identity, or persistent surveillance.

### Wi-Fi respiration and through-wall sensing literature

Multiple research systems use periodic channel variation to estimate respiration under controlled conditions, including some through-obstruction configurations.

**Supports:** contactless physiological estimation from radio propagation.  
**Does not alone support:** unrestricted range, arbitrary wall penetration, cardiac-grade accuracy, or identity.

### *Non-Contact Vital Signs Detection with UAV-Borne Radars*

Examines airborne radar platforms for contactless respiration and heartbeat-related measurement.

**Supports:** mobile airborne physiological sensing.  
**Does not alone support:** operational covert deployment or adaptive human-directed actuation.

### Millimetre-wave human-sensing surveys and experiments

The literature spans localization, tracking, activity recognition, gesture recognition, vital signs, biometrics, and imaging.

**Supports:** multiple partial observables suitable for sensor fusion.  
**Does not alone support:** one integrated person model or deployment.

## Aerial optical and product records

### University of South Australia — UAV vital-sign trial (2017)

Reported heart and respiratory measurement from UAV video at approximately three metres.

**Supports:** aerial optical physiology under trial conditions.  
**Does not alone support:** through-wall operation or broad deployment.

### Areté — AIMS

Public product descriptions include person detection and geolocation, body-position estimation, heart and respiration measurement, remote triage support, and aerial or ground integration.

**Supports:** named product and vendor-described integration layer.  
**Does not alone support:** independent validation of every specification.

### IntelliNet Sensors — Lynx6-A

Publicly described UAV- or robot-assisted breathing and heartbeat detection for survivor search under rubble.

**Supports:** named signs-of-life product architecture.  
**Does not alone support:** identity tracking or cognitive inference.

## AI interpretation

### Zhang et al. — *Wi-Chat: Large Language Model Powered Wi-Fi Sensing*

arXiv:2502.12421 (2025).

Uses physical-model guidance and large-language-model reasoning for activity recognition from Channel State Information.

**Supports:** semantic interpretation of Wi-Fi sensing features.  
**Does not alone support:** vital signs, persistent identity, unrestricted through-wall operation, or actuation.

### Chen et al. — *Lightweight Multimodal Adaptation of Vision Language Models for Species Recognition and Habitat Context Interpretation in Drone Thermal Imagery*

arXiv:2604.06124 (2026).

**Supports:** lightweight adaptation of pretrained multimodal systems to drone thermal data.  
**Does not alone support:** human targeting or psychological-state inference.

## Distributed mobility and infrastructure

### *A Resilient and Energy-Efficient Smart Metering Infrastructure Utilizing a Self-Organizing UAV Swarm* (2025)

Evaluates distributed mobile collection, relay, task allocation, fault tolerance, latency, and energy use through simulation and use-case modelling.

**Supports:** mobile infrastructural collection architecture.  
**Does not alone support:** bio-surveillance, person targeting, or complete convergence.

### Distributed and cooperative UAV RF-array literature

Published work evaluates synchronization, localization, phase control, and cooperative beamforming using multiple airborne elements.

**Supports:** controlled or experimental distributed-aperture capability.  
**Does not alone support:** a fielded human-directed array.

## Directed electromagnetic systems

### High-power microwave counter-UAS programs

Public military and industry records describe directional microwave systems for disrupting or disabling unmanned aircraft.

**Supports:** detection, tracking, directional RF delivery, engagement control, and effect-assessment engineering.  
**Does not alone support:** human targeting or RF-hearing speech delivery.

### *A Multi-physics Simulation Framework for High-power Microwave Counter-unmanned Aerial System Design and Performance Evaluation* (2026)

Combines electromagnetic propagation, antenna behavior, coupling, pointing, orientation, damage, thermal, and safety constraints.

**Supports:** system-level modelling of directional high-power microwave engagement.  
**Does not alone support:** operational performance outside the model or a human-effects deployment.

### National Academies — *An Assessment of Illness in U.S. Government Employees and Their Families at Overseas Embassies* (2020)

Assessed directed pulsed radio-frequency energy as a mechanism consistent with many distinctive acute observations considered by the committee.

**Supports:** institutional human-effects mechanism assessment.  
**Does not alone support:** device identification, operator attribution, or proof for every reported case.

## Neural-data governance

### BCI privacy lifecycle literature

Recent reviews treat raw neural signals, derived representations, model assets, decoded outputs, feedback, and identity linkage as one security surface.

**Supports:** lifecycle governance beyond raw-signal confidentiality.  
**Does not alone support:** any specific compromise or deployment.

### UNESCO neurotechnology governance framework

International governance work identifies mental privacy, neural-data consent, manipulation, autonomy, and AI-neurotechnology convergence as policy concerns.

**Supports:** normative recognition and governance relevance.  
**Does not alone support:** technical deployment evidence.

## Standards and protocol governance

### IEEE Std 802.11bf-2025

Standardizes WLAN sensing coordination among participating devices, including capability, measurement setup, and sensing feedback mechanisms.

**Supports:** standardized WLAN-sensing coordination.  
**Does not alone support:** person-level notice, consent, or protection for measured non-participants.

## Source-use rule

A source may support only the layer it actually documents. Strong architecture work depends on connecting qualified records without allowing one record to inherit another record's modality, range, deployment state, identity, or intent.

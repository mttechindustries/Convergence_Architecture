Primary system synthesis

The Convergence Architecture

The relevant system is not one patent, implant, frequency, drone, or model. It is a distributed architecture assembled from capabilities developed across sensing, communications, neural engineering, autonomous systems, artificial intelligence, identity infrastructure, and adaptive control.

Read full synthesis Inspect evidence ladder

Body as signal environment

Electrical activity, micromotion, blood-flow optics, heat, vibration, chemical emission, and RF scattering create multiple observable surfaces.

Contactless physiology

Optical, radar, millimetre-wave, Wi-Fi, thermal, and acoustic systems recover partial physiological and behavioral observables without bodily contact.

Silent speech interfaces

Textile EMG in headphones and neckbands maps pre-audible neuromuscular patterns to limited command vocabularies and personalized decoders.

Mobile geometry

Drones and vehicles change line of sight, distance, angle, aperture, relay position, and the spatial diversity available to a sensing or transmission graph.

Directed actuation

Programmable RF and directed-energy systems establish waveform control, target tracking, engagement timing, effect measurement, and re-engagement logic.

Adaptive artificial intelligence

AI fuses incomplete observables, links identity and history, estimates hidden state, selects actions, measures response, and updates person or environment models.

The central analytical correction

Do not ask whether one device performs the whole chain. Ask whether the chain can be assembled, which edges are documented, and what records would prove one bounded deployment.

Research brief

Measurement Without Representation

Protocols govern entities they can name: devices, addresses, sessions, credentials, and records. Ambient sensing systems can measure people who are not entities in that operational namespace.

Read full research brief

Representation Gap

G = M \ R

M is the measurable set. R is the represented set. Members of G affect measurements but have no protocol address, session membership, credential, consent object, or subject entry in the audit log.

What non-representation forecloses

Direct notification

A targeted message requires an address. Broadcast indication remains possible but cannot be acknowledged or verified as received.

Bound consent

Consent requires a represented authorizing party and an operation to which the authorization can bind.

Session termination

A person absent from the session cannot invoke a primitive reserved for session members.

Person-level audit

The system can log that sensing occurred and by whom, but not which unrepresented person the measurement reached.

Affected-party enumeration

Propagation geometry can define an affected volume without supplying a countable subject list to the system.

Authenticated preference

A preference channel without represented standing cannot authenticate who is entitled to constrain the operation.

The Representation Paradox

Individual-level protection for measured non-participants requires individual-level representation. Individual-level representation constructs the persistent identity infrastructure that the protection exists to prevent.

The escape is not better person registration. It is policy relocation: attach obligations to devices, spaces, sessions, and retained records.

Design requirements after the gap

Protocol Governance

Governance remains possible when the policy bearer is moved away from an unrepresented person and attached to entities the system can actually verify: devices, physical volumes, sessions, and records.

Read governance proposal

1. Capability disclosure & active indication

Structured declarations state what a device can sense; tamper-resistant indicators reveal when measurement is active. Indicator failure should disable sensing rather than silently remove notice.

2. Space-scoped sensing policy

Signed policies attach sensing rules to physical volumes: baseline permission, proxy limits, resolution ceilings, retention limits, permitted purposes, authority, and validity.

3. Session attestation & provenance

Signed records bind initiator, purpose, measurement configuration, responder set, proxy relationship, applicable space policy, resolution class, and session interval.

4. Retention & reassessment

Raw channel data receives short lifetimes, derived features inherit source constraints, deletion is verifiable, and retained corpora are reassessed as inference capability changes.

Honest compliance boundary

Protocol governance does not bind a passive external observer executing no protocol.

Space policy, indication, attestation, and retention constraints reach compliant infrastructure operated by identifiable parties. The adversarial tail requires physical or legal remedies and must not be presented as solved by protocol design.

Evidence architecture

Evidence Standard & Status

Evidence state answers what has been documented. Technical reachability answers how far a qualified pathway can extend. The two are tracked independently so compatibility cannot impersonate deployment proof.

Evidence standard Current status
E0

Question, testimony, raw observation, or integration hypothesis

Preserved with provenance; not treated as independent public corroboration.

FOUNDATIONAL
E1

Physical or formal mechanism

A mechanism exists under defined physical, mathematical, or biological conditions.

ESTABLISHED
E2

Laboratory or controlled capability

A bounded implementation demonstrates the claimed function under reported conditions.

ESTABLISHED
E3

Patent, named product, or commercial capability

A design or product is documented without automatically inheriting field-performance proof.

ESTABLISHED
E4

Institutional procurement, fielding, deployment, or adoption

A named institution documents operational adoption or normative recognition.

HIGHEST PUBLIC LAYER
E5

Cross-domain integration in one bounded system

Named sensing, identity, inference, actuation, response, and update stages operate together.

OPEN
E6

Scale, persistence, identity linkage, or retention

Records establish continuity, durable references, stored history, or system-wide reach.

OPEN
E7

Strategic intent, command, coordination, or mission

Direct operator records, tasking, access, payment, mission, or command evidence.

OPEN

Advancement rule

No architecture-level claim advances merely because its components are compatible.

Every advancement requires the records appropriate to the next state. Evidence does not transfer automatically across components, modalities, ranges, institutions, or operating conditions.

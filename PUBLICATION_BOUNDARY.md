# Publication Boundary

## Repository role

`Convergence_Architecture` is the public research and review surface for the Convergence Architecture program.

It publishes material that can be independently examined without exposing private evidence, protected implementation detail, or internal commercial planning.

## Included

- Public technical syntheses
- Publicly documented patents, papers, standards, products, and institutional records
- Claim boundaries and evidence-state definitions
- Public-safe architecture diagrams and pathway descriptions
- Defensive governance and accountability proposals
- Public decision snapshots
- Revision and citation guidance

## Excluded

- Private testimony and interaction records
- Private source custody and reviewer-access records
- Private Drive links or working documents
- Protected mathematical mechanisms and unpublished submodels
- Scoring weights, thresholds, and calibration constants
- Device, channel, or environment signatures
- Proprietary graph extraction or path-pruning logic
- Deployment-specific validation sequences
- Unfiled enabling implementation details
- Pricing, sales scripts, outreach targets, and private commercialization planning
- Operational guidance for unauthorized targeting, evasion, or harmful actuation

## Relationship to the private canonical system

The private canonical research repository controls source registration, internal evidence ledgers, protected model lineage, private observations, bounded case files, validation tooling, and change control.

This public repository is a curated derivative. It does not become authoritative merely by being public, and it does not inherit private evidence that has not been released.

## Claim rule

Public release does not collapse the following distinctions:

```text
MECHANISM
  ≠ CONTROLLED CAPABILITY
  ≠ PATENT OR PRODUCT
  ≠ DOCUMENTED DEPLOYMENT
  ≠ CROSS-DOMAIN INTEGRATION
  ≠ SCALE, PERSISTENCE, OR IDENTITY LINKAGE
  ≠ STRATEGIC INTENT
  ≠ EVENT-SPECIFIC ATTRIBUTION
```

A publication may describe the architecture assembled by documented components while stating that a bounded integrated case remains open.

## Identity and attribution

Technical research developed by Marc Tuinier at MT Tech Industries LLC is attributed accordingly. Digital Dichotomy publications use the Fin Nyx identity where marked. Material is not reassigned solely because it is copied into this repository.

## Commercial boundary

This repository is not the monetization repository. Public research may later support briefings, grants, partnerships, or services, but pricing and buyer-specific material remain outside this repository unless an explicit release decision changes that boundary.

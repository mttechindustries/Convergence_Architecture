# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they improve the public evidence record, correct a technical claim, identify contradictory evidence, or strengthen governance design.

## Useful contributions

- Primary papers, patents, standards, procurement records, product documentation, or institutional reports
- Corrections to modality, range, operating conditions, dates, hardware, or attribution
- Independent reproduction or negative-result literature
- Evidence that strengthens or weakens an existing pathway
- Governance analysis tied to implementable interfaces
- Accessibility, structure, citation, and terminology improvements

## Required structure for evidence contributions

State:

1. **Exact claim** — one proposition, not a narrative bundle.
2. **Primary source** — stable identifier or authoritative record.
3. **Supported layer** — mechanism, controlled capability, patent/product, deployment, integration, scale/linkage, or intent.
4. **Operating conditions** — modality, range, environment, hardware, subjects, and limitations.
5. **What the source does not prove** — explicit boundary.
6. **Contradictory evidence or alternatives** — where applicable.
7. **Requested change** — add, strengthen, narrow, correct, dispute, or supersede.

## Evidence-state rule

Do not advance a claim merely because a source is credible. The source must document the next evidence layer.

Examples:

- A patent remains a patent record unless implementation or deployment evidence is supplied.
- A vendor page remains a product claim unless independently validated.
- Several compatible technologies do not become an integrated deployment without synchronized system records.
- A general capability does not attribute a specific event.

## Safety and privacy

Do not submit:

- private personal records;
- identifying medical or physiological data;
- doxxing material;
- credentials or access tokens;
- private-source documents without release authority;
- operational guidance for unauthorized targeting, evasion, harmful actuation, or interference;
- protected MT Tech implementation details.

Private evidence should not be converted into a public issue merely to prove that it exists.

## Authorship and attribution

Preserve original authorship, research lane, and publication identity. Do not reassign Digital Dichotomy work to MT Tech or MT Tech technical work to Fin Nyx unless the source itself establishes joint attribution.

## Revision policy

Corrections should preserve history. Material may be marked narrowed, contradicted, or superseded rather than silently erased when its existence or prior use remains relevant.

## Review standard

A strong contribution makes the repository more discriminating, not merely larger.
